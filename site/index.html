YOUR TIME

MyCal

Make space for your day.

A personal calendar for plans big and small. Organize events, see your month at a glance, and bring your calendars along with iCalendar support.

  • See the big picture or the next appointment with year, month, week, day, and schedule views.
  • Keep plans easy to scan with color-coded events.
  • Import and export iCalendar files, and share a calendar feed with other calendar apps.

YOUR CONVERSATIONS

MyMail

Give your inbox a little clarity.

A personal email client. Keep conversations together, find messages with full-text search, and make use of scheduled sending and snooze.

  • Follow threaded conversations and compose rich-text messages with attachments.
  • Organize mail with folders and delivery filters; narrow searches by sender, recipient, or date.
  • Schedule sending for later, snooze a message, and use different sending identities and signatures.

YOUR IDEAS

MyNotes

Catch a thought. Let it grow.

A home for quick thoughts and longer ideas. Write in Markdown, connect your notes, and give your thinking room to take shape.

  • Go beyond plain text with images, tables, diagrams, math, and callouts.
  • Organize with tags, search your collection, and connect ideas with links between notes.
  • Import and export Markdown files, or download a note as a standalone HTML document.

YOUR FINDS

MyLinks

Good finds deserve a home.

Save the pages you want to come back to. Collect bookmarks with automatic titles and descriptions, then search your saved pages when you need them.

  • Save a page without leaving it using the browser bookmarklet.
  • Search titles, descriptions, and the text of saved pages.
  • Keep short notes alongside your bookmarks.
  • Capture screenshots of saved pages with the Docker setup.

YOUR PASSWORDS

MyPass

One less thing to remember.

A password manager that keeps your passwords in an encrypted file. Generate and retrieve passwords from the command line, with an Android app for your phone.

  • Generate passwords with your choice of length and character set.
  • Copy a password when you need it, with a configurable clipboard-clearing timeout.
  • Use the Firefox integration to fill login forms on sites you have associated with your entries.
